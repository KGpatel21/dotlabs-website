1. WHY "VERIFY TOKENS IN THE APPS SCRIPT" — even though Cloudflare is on
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Short answer: YES, still do it. 2 minutes. Here's why.

The Turnstile widget on your forms only runs INSIDE the browser, on your page.
But your Google Apps Script webhook URL is public — a spam bot doesn't have to
visit your website at all. It can POST junk data straight to the webhook URL
and skip the widget, the honeypot, and the time-trap entirely.

  Without verification:
    Bot ──────────────────────────────► Apps Script ► your sheet + email  ❌

  With verification:
    Bot (no valid token) ─────────────► Apps Script ► REJECTED            ✅
    Human (widget passed, token sent) ► Apps Script ► verified ► saved    ✅

The widget ISSUES a token; only your Apps Script can CHECK it (by calling
Cloudflare with your SECRET key). That's why the secret belongs in the Apps
Script — Vercel never verifies anything because your site is static.

Do this (from SECURITY_TURNSTILE.md):
  1. Open your sheet → Extensions → Apps Script
  2. Paste the verifyTurnstile() function, put your SECRET key in it
  3. Add at the top of doPost:
       if (!verifyTurnstile(p.turnstileToken)) {
         return ContentService.createTextOutput("blocked");
       }
  4. Deploy → Manage deployments → Edit (pencil) → New version
     (NOT "New deployment" — that would change your URL)

Until you do it, you're still protected from casual bots (honeypot+time-trap),
just not from bots that target the webhook directly.

4. GOOGLE SEARCH CONSOLE + BING — get indexed
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  1. search.google.com/search-console → "Add property" → choose **Domain**
     → enter: sparkentechnologies.com
  2. Google shows a TXT record like  google-site-verification=abc123...
  3. Add that TXT record where your DNS lives:
     - If your DNS is at your REGISTRAR (GoDaddy/Namecheap/etc.): DNS settings
       → Add record → Type TXT → Host @ → Value = the string → Save.
     - If you moved DNS to VERCEL: Vercel → Domains → sparkentechnologies.com
       → DNS Records → Add → TXT @ → paste value.
  4. Back in Search Console → "Verify" (may take a few minutes for DNS).
  5. Left menu → Sitemaps → enter  sitemap.xml  → Submit.
     (Full URL: https://www.sparkentechnologies.com/sitemap.xml)
  6. Optional turbo: URL Inspection → paste your homepage URL → "Request
     indexing". Repeat for /services and /hire-developers.
  7. BING: bing.com/webmasters → Sign in → "Import from Google Search
     Console" → one click, done. (Bing also feeds DuckDuckGo & Yahoo.)
  Expect: indexed within days; meaningful rankings take weeks — the directory
  listings (Clutch/GoodFirms) and blog posts accelerate it.
