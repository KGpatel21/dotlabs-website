HOW TO USE
1. Copy the STYLE BLOCK below and paste it at the END of every prompt.
   This keeps all images looking like one consistent brand family.
2. Generate at the largest size Gemini offers, then download the original
   (not the preview). Target at least 2560px on the long edge; upscale to
   4K with Gemini's upscale option or any HD upscaler before uploading.
3. Save as WebP (or high-quality JPG) before adding to the site — smaller
   files, same quality.
4. IMPORTANT: The prompts say "no text, no words, no logos". AI-generated
   text always looks broken — headlines belong in HTML, not in the image.
